# Hackathon brief: Builders Hack: Unlocked

Lyra × Rampersand's Product Counsel. Event page: https://luma.com/e2qpol61
Submission form: https://www.productcounsel.com.au/builders-hack#submissions

## Brief

**Give someone a new ability.** Pick one kind of person and one useful task they can't
currently complete themselves. Build something that gets them from "I wouldn't know where to
start" to "I just did that." Show that the problem is big and affects many people.

Weekend steps:
1. **Understand the person.** Talk to someone who has the problem, or use lived experience or credible first-hand accounts.
2. **Choose the ability.** Make it small enough to build in a weekend, but rooted in a big problem.
3. **Build it** end to end. Use AI in the build, in the product, or both, and explain what it contributed.
4. **Hand it over.** Have someone unfamiliar try the task. Report where they got stuck and what
   you changed. If you used a proxy tester or a simulated scenario, say so.

Use permissioned, anonymised or synthetic examples, and label them clearly.

## Deliverables (all three are required)

1. **Public website** that speaks directly to the target person: what the product does, what it
   unlocks for them, and a clear next step.
2. **Four-page deck:**
   1. Who is it for, and how big is the problem (with evidence)?
   2. What can they do now (the new ability)?
   3. What did you learn (the evidence behind your choices, and an assumption you tested or changed)?
   4. What is real today (what was built, how AI was used, what works, what's simulated or done manually,
      what still needs work, and what was made during the hackathon versus what already existed)?
3. **Demo**, 5 minutes or less, in any format. Show the ability being unlocked.

Submission: 1 to 5 links, one per line, covering all three. Judges must be able to open them without requesting access.
Teams of 1 to 4. **Every team member must complete the Builders Survey**
(https://www.productcounsel.com.au/the-builders-survey) before submitting.

## Judging (equally weighted)

| Criterion | What judges look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Problem** | Who it's for, what blocks them, how they manage today, evidence of scale, and what you learned. |
| **Product** | Fit for purpose, whether the core experience works, craft, what was tested, what works and what still needs work. |
| **Presentation** | Clear and compelling story across the website, deck and demo; the product shown in action; clear about what's real and what's simulated. |

Central question: **What can this person now do that they couldn't do before? Show us.**
